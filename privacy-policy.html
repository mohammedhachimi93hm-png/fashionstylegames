Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fashion Style Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the games we publish on the Amazon Appstore (the "Games") and on this website, fashionstylegames.com (the "Website").

In short: our Games do not collect, store or share any personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through our Games. Our Games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and they do not require an account or login.

Game progress and settings (for example unlocked items or sound settings) are saved only on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Advertising and analytics

Our Games do not show advertising and do not contain advertising SDKs. Our Games do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not build user profiles.

3. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone.

4. Amazon Appstore

Our Games are downloaded through the Amazon Appstore. Any information you give to Amazon (for example your Amazon account or payment details) is handled by Amazon under Amazon's own privacy notice. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Children's privacy

Our Games are made to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. This Website

This Website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not load third-party fonts or advertising. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically record technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and service reasons. This is handled by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy statement.

7. Emails you send us

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to answer you, and we do not share it. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

8. Data deletion

Since our Games keep data only on your device, you can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in your device settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@fashionstylegames.com.
